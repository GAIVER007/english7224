SpeakUp offline translation

Models: OPUS-MT Russian <-> English (Marian), converted to ONNX by the ONNX Community.
Original models: Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-ru-en and Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-ru.
ONNX model repositories:
https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/opus-mt-ru-en
https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/opus-mt-en-ru
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Weights are downloaded from the pinned ONNX repository revisions. SpeakUp does not alter them.

OPUS-MT authors: Jorg Tiedemann and Santhosh Thottingal.
Reference: OPUS-MT -- Building open translation services for the World (2020).
https://github.com/Helsinki-NLP/Opus-MT

Runtime: Hugging Face Transformers.js (Apache-2.0), ONNX Runtime (MIT).
Translation is performed locally in a browser Web Worker. Text is not sent to a translation API.
